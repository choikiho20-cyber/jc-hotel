JC Hotel Jecheon

To stay in the
middle of the city.

JC Hotel sits in the middle of downtown Jecheon. The concert hall is a minute away on foot and dinner is in the building next door. You can park the car and walk the city.

We reopened in September 2024 with every room renovated. The breakfast has made a name for itself in Jecheon, and a business room on the first floor serves guests here on work.

FAQ

Frequently asked

The questions guests ask most often before booking.

Is parking available?

Yes. We have our own car park and it is free for guests. We do not offer valet parking, so you park yourself.

What time is breakfast?

Buffet breakfast is served at CAFE jc on the first floor, 07:00 to 08:30. It is closed on Mondays.

How much is breakfast?

KRW 15,000 per person. Add it when you book, or pay at the hotel. Terms differ by booking channel, so please confirm with the front desk.

Is there a sauna or gym?

No. Older listings on some booking sites may say otherwise.

Can I leave my luggage?

Yes. The front desk holds bags, including after checkout.

Is there a computer in the room?

Not in standard rooms. Two work PCs and a printer are in the first-floor lounge, and the Deluxe 2PC room type has PCs in the room.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 15:00 and check-out is 11:00. Please call the front desk if you arrive after 22:00.

Do you have Korean ondol rooms?

Yes. We run an Ondol Room for 4 and an Ondol Room for 8; the larger one sleeps up to 10 guests.

Are there any discounts?

The Digital Tourist Residency Card gives 10% off the room rate, and paying with the Jecheon Moa Card adds local currency benefits.
